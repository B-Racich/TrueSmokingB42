title=True Smoking [B42]
description=[h1] True Smoking [B42] [/h1]
description=[code][h3]This mod provides a more realistic and immersive smoking experience. Smoking is no longer a short action but now a tracked and passively handled mechanic where the stats and effects are distributed over time while smoking. There is also a optional Nicotine System that implements Addiction and Withdrawal[/h3][/code]
description=
description=[h1]Smoking[/h1]
description=[code]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/WBnGq4I.gif[/img]
description=Light a smoke as normal, any restrictive headgear will be taken off. After lighting, a moodle will appear tracking the progress of your smoke. You can take puffs manually or enable passive smoking and your character will take puffs when idle.
description=[/code]
description=
description=[h1]Dynamic Burn Rate[/h1]
description=[code]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/QLxA9Ts.gif[/img]
description=The smoke will burn faster as you puff or move around, when idle it will slowly go out. If it goes out you can relight it to keep smoking. Smokes can be put out and saved for later, the amount left will be visible on the item tooltip.
description=[/code]
description=
description=[h1]Freedom[/h1]
description=[code]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/vQdNBdG.gif[/img]
description=Lighting, puffing, and putting out the smoke are actions but while smoking you can do anything else now!
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/bkegWkz.gif[/img]
description=[/code]
description=

description=[h3]Controls[/h3]
description=[code]
description=[h3]Default Keyboard[/h3]
description=[b]K[/b] - Tap to take a short puff, hold to keep puffing. Will light a cigarette or unpack one. Relights the smoke when if it goes out.
description=[b]; (semi colon)[/b] - Puts the smoke out. Re-equips headgear that was taken off.
description=
description=[h3]Controller[/h3]
description=Button prompts will appear when the corresponding action can be done. LB is a modifier key that when held changes the B action as follows below:
description=LB + B = Get/Light Cigarette
description=LB + B = Put Out Smoke / Re-equip headgear
description=B = (Hold) Take Puff / Relight
description=[/code]
description=
description=[h3] Configurable[/h3]
description=[code]
description=[h3]Smokable Options[/h3]
description=All vanilla items are configurable from the sandbox options to tweak their characteristics to your desired behaviour. Smoke faster/longer, make them burn faster/slower, etc.
description=
description=[h3]Smoke Relighting[/h3]
description=Smoke relighting can be toggled to never have the smoke go out and require relighting.
description=
description=[h3]Headgear Management / Display Smoke On Mouth[/h3]
description=Headgear management will toggle the need for removing headgear and also showing a visual smoke in the players mouth.
description=
description=[h3]Automatic Puffing[/h3]
description=You can setup automatic puffing to trigger between an interval and/or to always attempt to take a puff when the smoke is going out
description=
description=[h3]Unpack Cigarette / Light[/h3]
description=The unpacking and lighting feature can be toggled off if desired if using another mod that does something similar. Currently it only supports the vanilla cigarette pack and cigarettes.
description=
description=[h3]More Settings[/h3]
description=I add new settings as requested and there are more than listed here and shown in the screenshots. I am open to making most things configurable where I can and adding in new features/settings where it makes sense.
description=[/code]
