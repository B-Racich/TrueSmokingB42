title=True Smoking [B42]
description=[h1] True Smoking [B42] [/h1]
description=[code]
description=This mod provides a more realistic and immersive smoking experience. Smoking is no longer a short action but now a tracked and passively handled mechanic. 
description=
description=When you 'Smoke' something the item will be used, your player will perform a quick 'lighting' action and the Smoking Moodle will appear showing you the status of your smoke! The burn rate will fluctuate as you smoke and you will need to keep puffing to keep your smoke lit!
description=
description=The stats from smoking and the item will be distributed to your character as you smoke, keep puffing to smoke faster, puff less to smoke slower. If the smoke goes out the Moodle will wiggle and you can relight it to keep smoking. If you are running or sprinting the smoke will go out quicker and you are not able to take puffs.
description=[/code]
description=[h3] Features [/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*]Moodle tracks your smoke after lighting
description=[*]Smoke burns faster or slower depending on how much you puff and if you are idle/running/sprinting
description=[*]Relight the smoke if it goes out (Moodle will wiggle)
description=[*]Stat effects are now distributed over the duration of the smoke
description=[*]Compatible with Smoker Trait
description=[*]Hotkey for unpacking and lighting a cigarette
description=[*]Different smoke times for different items (cigars take longer than cigarettes)
description=[*]Options and Settings configurable via Sandbox Options and Mod Options
description=[*]Automatic passive puffing (configurable in options)
description=[*]Coughing mechanic configurable in sandbox options (chance to cough on taking a puff)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h3] Default Keybinds [/h3]
description=Press [b]K[/b] to take puffs and to relight your smoke if it goes out, finds a cigarette and lights it if not smoking
description=Press [b]; (semi colon)[/b] to stop smoking
description=
description=[h3] Options [/h3]
description=To configure smoking times change the Sandbox options for the item Lengths i.e Cigarette Length. Adjust this to your preference but take note; the longer the smoking time the longer it takes for you to get the full effect from the smoke. In terms of balance items like cigars which give more benefit should take longer to smoke, not just for realism but to distribute the bigger chunk of stat changes over a longer time.
description=
description=You can enable the override toggle to have all smokable items set at one smoke length defined in the Sandbox options.
description=
description=The automatic passive puffing feature lets your character automatically puff when idle, the frequency of this can be adjusted or turned off.
description=

description=[h3] General Notes On Balance [/h3]
description=[code]
description=I've respected the vanilla logic as closely as possible and mods that are compatible work as expected but there is a few differences to note.
description=
description=Since stats are distributed over time, small stat changes from mods may be harder to notice if its a slight fatigue change or something similar but they are still taking affect! This is something I can look at closer on my end to try and balance but I'd rather leave balancing to the mod authors themselves and create a system that works well.
description=
description=Some may find the smoking times too short or too long but that is why I added options to configure it, I'll likely tweak these over time with more feedback and play testing. I've added support for modders to define this in their items as well so they can also balance it as they see fit.
description=
description=One creative liberty I took was changing how the new coughing mechanic works. In vanilla smokers have a greater chance at coughing than non smokers but with how I handle smoking it made more sense to me to give smokers a lower chance at coughing than non-smokers as its now based on each puff taken and not consuming the whole smoke at once. You can turn this off completely or adjust the values in the sandbox for it to configure it either way.
description=[/code]
description=[h3] Note For Modders [/h3]
description=[code]
description=I have added logic to check for a 'smokeLength' value in items like so[code]item:getModData().smokeLength[/code]if this is added my mod should use your value set instead of the sandbox or predefined values. Feel free to reach out to me if you want to add support for this.
