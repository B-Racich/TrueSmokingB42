title=True Smoking [B42]
description=[h1] True Smoking [B42] [/h1]
description=[code]
description=This mod provides a more realistic and immersive smoking experience. Smoking is no longer a short action but now a tracked and passively handled mechanic. 
description=
description=Smoking starts as normal, you light a smoke but after the initial action the Smoking Moodle will appear and you will see the smoke in your players mouth. You can manually take puffs and the player will grab the smoke from their mouth and puff as long as you like.
description=
description=The smoke has a dynamic burn rate, the less you puff the slower it burns until it goes out, if it does you have to relight it to keep smoking.
description=
description=You can put out your smoke at any time but unless you smoke the whole thing you wont get the full affect. (You can not save a partially smoked item...yet)
description=[/code]
description=[h3] Features [/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Save Game Compatible![/b]
description=[*]Moodle tracks your smoke after lighting
description=[*]Dynamic burn rate for the smoke
description=[*]Relighting
description=[*]Stat effects are now distributed over the duration of the smoke
description=[*]Compatible with Smoker Trait
description=[*]Hotkey for unpacking and lighting a cigarette
description=[*]Different smoke times for different items (cigars take longer than cigarettes)
description=[*]Options and Settings configurable via Sandbox Options and Mod Options
description=[*]Automatic passive puffing (configurable in options)
description=[*]Coughing mechanic configurable in sandbox options (chance to cough on taking a puff)
description=[*]Visible smoke in mouth while smoking!
description=[*]Headgear management (anything that restricts smoking is unequipped and re-equipped)
description=[*]Controller support
description=[*]Split-screen support
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h3]Note on puffing actions[/h3]
description=If you played earlier versions of my mod there were no restrictions on how 'short' a puff could be, since the latest update I have now added a minimum time to the puffs to ensure everything is sync'd for displaying the smoke from the players hand and mouth. If you are holding the puff key for awhile and let go, it will wait till it can sync the animation to the mouth to return the cigarette. Additionally if you tap the key there is a minimum length to the puff animation that it attempts. Running/sprinting/aiming should cancel the animation as expected if you need to and everything should be fine, the smoke will just appear back into the mouth in that case.
description=
description=[h3] Default Keybinds [/h3]
description=Press [b]K[/b] to take puffs and to relight your smoke if it goes out, finds a cigarette and lights it if not smoking
description=Press [b]; (semi colon)[/b] to stop smoking (Also attempts to re-equip headgear that was taken off if the action was cancelled)
description=
description=[h3] Controllers [/h3]
description=I've added button prompts that display when you can do the following action.  LB is a modifier key that when held changes the B action as follows below:
description=LB + B = Get/Light Cigarette
description=LB + B = Put Out Smoke
description=B = (Hold) Take Puff / Relight
description=
description=Eventually i'll make these configurable in the options, let me know if there is major issues with these controller binds
description=
description=[h3] Options [/h3]
description=To configure smoking times change the Sandbox options for the item Lengths i.e Cigarette Length. Adjust this to your preference but take note; the longer the smoking time the longer it takes for you to get the full effect from the smoke. In terms of balance items like cigars which give more benefit should take longer to smoke, not just for realism but to distribute the bigger chunk of stat changes over a longer time.
description=
description=You can enable the override toggle to have all smokable items set at one smoke length defined in the Sandbox options.
description=
description=SmokeRelighting can be toggled to never have the smoke go out
description=
description=Headgear management will toggle the need for removing headgear and also showing a visual smoke in the players mouth
description=
description=The automatic passive puffing feature lets your character automatically puff when idle, the frequency of this can be adjusted or turned off.
description=
