title=True Smoking [B42]
description=[h1] True Smoking [B42] [/h1]
description=
description=[h3]Updated for B42![/h3]
description=
description=This mod overhauls the smoking mechanic to make it more immersive, providing longer smoking times, manual puffing of your smoke, and dynamically calculating the burn rate as you smoke faster or slower. This mod is save compatible and will work with MP when it releases.
description=
description=[h3] Features [/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*]Moodle tracks your smoke after lighting
description=[*]Smoke burns faster or slower depending on how much you puff
description=[*]Relight the smoke if it goes out
description=[*]Stat effects are now distributed over the duration of the smoke
description=[*]Compatible with Smoker Trait
description=[*]Hotkey for unpacking and lighting a cigarette
description=[*]Different smoke times for different items (cigars take longer than cigarettes)
description=[*]Options and Settings configurable via Sandbox Options and Mod Options
description=[*]Automatic passive puffing (configurable in options)
description=[*]Coughing mechanic configurable in sandbox options (chance to cough on taking a puff)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h3] Default Keybinds [/h3]
description=Press [b]K[/b] to take puffs and to relight your smoke if it goes out, finds a cigarette and lights it if not smoking
description=Press [b]; (colon)[/b] to stop smoking
description=
description=[h3] Options [/h3]
description=If you want to adjust how long it takes to smoke something change the values in the sandbox options for the different item lengths (Cigarette Length, Cigar Length, etc)
description=Passive Smoking when enabled will allow the player to automatically take puffs based on the user defined interval
description=
