title=True Smoking [B42]
description=[h1] True Smoking [B42] [/h1]
description=[code][b]This mod provides a more realistic and immersive smoking experience. Smoking is no longer a short action but now a tracked and passively handled mechanic.[/b][/code]
description=
description=[code]
description=[h1]Smoking[/h1]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/XuqrLAH.gif[/img]
description=Smoking starts as normal, you light a smoke but after the initial action the Smoking Moodle will appear and you will see the smoke in your players mouth. You can manually take puffs and the player will grab the smoke from their mouth and puff as long as you like. You can put out your smoke at any time but unless you smoke the whole thing you wont get the full affect. [b]NEW![/b] When putting out a smoke it now saves how much is left, and you can smoke the rest later!
description=[/code]
description=
description=[code]
description=[h1]Dynamic Burn Rate[/h1]
description=[img]https://i.ibb.co/qY7qsNyF/Relighting.gif[/img]
description=The smoke has a dynamic burn rate, the less you puff the slower it burns until it goes out, if it does you have to relight it to keep smoking.
description=[/code]
description=
description=[code]
description=[h1]Freedom[/h1]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/vQdNBdG.gif[/img]
description=Once you begin smoking you are free to do other things, drive, fight zombies, read books, cook, etc. You just won't be able to take puffs if you are performing another action (the bar above the players head).
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/bkegWkz.gif[/img]
description=[/code]

description=[h3] Features [/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Save Game Compatible![/b]
description=[*]Controller support
description=[*]Split-screen support
description=[*][b]Partial smoking, save the rest of your smoke for later if you want! (Shown in tooltip)[/b]
description=[*]Moodle to track smoke
description=[*]Dynamic burn rate, relighting, manual puffing, [b]visible smoke on character mouth[/b]
description=[*]Stat effects are now distributed over the duration of the smoke
description=[*]Compatible with Smoker Trait
description=[*]Configurable hotkeys
description=[*]Different items have different smoke lengths and take more or less time to smoke
description=[*]Options and Settings configurable via Sandbox Options and Mod Options
description=[*]Automatic passive puffing (configurable in options)
description=[*]Coughing mechanic configurable in sandbox options (chance to cough on taking a puff)
description=[*]Headgear management (anything that restricts smoking is unequipped and re-equipped after smoking)
description=[/list]

description=[h3]Controls[/h3]
description=[code]
description=[h3]Keyboard[/h3]
description=[b]K[/b] - Tap to take a short puff, hold to keep puffing. Will light a cigarette or unpack one. Relights the smoke when if it goes out.
description=[b]; (semi colon)[/b] - Puts the smoke out. Re-equips headgear that was taken off.
description=
description=[h3]Controller[/h3]
description=Button prompts will appear when the corresponding action can be done. LB is a modifier key that when held changes the B action as follows below:
description=LB + B = Get/Light Cigarette
description=LB + B = Put Out Smoke / Re-equip headgear
description=B = (Hold) Take Puff / Relight
description=
description=These will be configurable at some point.
description=[/code]
description=
description=[h1]I update frequently and balance the default values as I develop the mod, if using the default settings consider removing the mod from your save and re-adding it to update the default values for this mod, or using a mod to change your sandbox settings[/h1]
description=
description=[h3] Options [/h3]
description=[code]
description=[h3]Smoke Length[/h3]
description=Smoke Length adjusts how long it takes to smoke something.You can set a default and toggle an override to make all items the same smoke length.
description=
description=[h3]Puff/Running/Idle Factors[/h3]
description=Adjust these values in the sandbox to change how much the burnRate changes during these actions
description=
description=[h3]Smoke Relighting[/h3]
description=Smoke relighting can be toggled to never have the smoke go out
description=
description=[h3]Headgear Management / Display Smoke On Mouth[/h3]
description=Headgear management will toggle the need for removing headgear and also showing a visual smoke in the players mouth.
description=
description=[h3]Automatic Passive Puffing[/h3]
description=The automatic passive puffing feature lets your character automatically puff when idle, the frequency of this can be adjusted or turned off.
description=
description=[h3]Unpack Cigarette / Light[/h3]
description=The unpacking and lighting feature can be toggled off if desired if using another mod that does something similar.
description=[/code]
